One live room for you and your friends.

Atti chat is a place to talk, play and sketch together, so a conversation with a friend never has to move to another app to turn into something fun.

What you can do here

Find a friend by their exact username and message them directly, or name a group and add everyone. Anyone in a group can rename it or add more people later, and you can see when your messages have been seen.

Challenge someone to Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Memory Match, Rock Paper Scissors or a Reaction Duel without leaving the conversation. Open a shared canvas and draw together. Turn on disappear mode and your messages delete themselves after ten seconds. Turn on notifications and your phone or laptop alerts you when a friend writes.

Why it exists

Most chat apps are for sending messages, and most games are for playing alone or with strangers. Atti chat puts the two together for people who already know each other. Quick games, a shared sketchpad and a live chat sit in one room, so winning a round of Tic-Tac-Toe and arguing about it happen in the same place.

Atti chat is kept small on purpose. You find people by username, not by a public directory, and there is no feed to scroll.

How it is paid for

Running a live chat costs money for hosting and storage. To help cover it, Atti chat may show ads from Google and other ad networks. You can read exactly how ads and cookies work in the privacy policy.

Who builds it
