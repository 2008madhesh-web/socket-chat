Privacy policy

What Atti chat keeps, who can see it, how ads and cookies work, and how to get your data deleted.

Last updated November 11, 2026

On this page

On this page

The short version

What we keep
Your username and a hashed password, or your Google name and picture. Your direct and group chats. The settings you choose.
Who can read your messages
The people in each chat. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are stored on our servers so you can read them again.
What others can see
Whether you are online, when you are typing, and when you have seen their messages.
Ads
Atti chat may show ads served by Google and other third parties. They use cookies, including Google’s DART cookie, to choose ads. You can opt out of personalized ads.
Deleting your data
Email 2008madhesh@gmail.com and we will delete your account and messages.

Who we are

Atti chat (attichat.madhesh.website) is a chat app built and run by Madhesh. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Atti chat and the person who runs it. You can reach us at 2008madhesh@gmail.com or on the contact page.

What we collect

When you create an account

If you sign up with a username, we store the username, the display name you choose and a hash of your password. We never store the password itself. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, your name and your profile picture. We ask for the “profile” permission only, so we do not receive your email address or contacts.

What you write and do in the app

Messages in direct messages and groups are stored in our database, along with the time they were sent, who sent them, any reaction added to them and the message being replied to. The public room is kept in the server’s memory only and is cleared when the server restarts. Games and drawings exist only while they are open. When a game ends, a one-line result such as who won is left in the chat.

Disappearing messages are deleted from our database ten seconds after they are sent.

Information about when you are around

So that chats feel live, the people you chat with can see whether you are online, when you are typing, and when you have seen their messages. In a direct message or group, every member can see which members have seen a message and at what time. Opening a chat while the app is in front of you counts as seeing its messages.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription: an address on your browser maker’s push service and two keys. We store it only to send you message alerts. Turn notifications off in the settings menu and we delete it.

Technical information

Like any website, our servers and hosting provider receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We use this to keep the service running, limit abuse and fix problems.

Cookies and local storage

Atti chat uses its own cookie and browser storage only for things the app needs:

  • atti.sid is a session cookie that keeps you logged in. It is not readable by scripts on the page and expires after 14 days.
  • Local storage in your browser remembers choices such as light or dark mode and whether you turned notifications on. It never leaves your device unless you use the feature it belongs to.

Advertising partners set their own cookies. These are described in the next section.

Advertising, Google cookies and third-party ad serving

Atti chat may show ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. Ads help pay for hosting. The companies that serve them are separate from us and have their own privacy policies.

Google advertising cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to Atti chat or to other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and to other sites on the Internet.

The DART cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses the DART cookie (part of its DoubleClick advertising technology) to serve ads to you based on your visits to Atti chat and other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

Other third-party ad servers and ad networks

Third-party ad servers and ad networks use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript and web beacons in the ads and links that appear on Atti chat. These are sent directly to your browser, and the ad network automatically receives your IP address when this happens. They are used to measure how well advertising works and to personalize the ads you see. Atti chat has no access to or control over the cookies used by third-party advertisers.

Your choices about ads

  • Turn off personalized ads from Google in Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising from other participating vendors at aboutads.info.
  • See how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Atti chat keeps working, but you will need to log in again.

Where the law requires your consent before personalized ads are shown, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, ads are personalized only if you agree. We do not share the content of your messages with advertisers and do not use it to choose ads.

How we use information

  • To create your account, log you in and deliver your messages, games and drawings.
  • To show online status, typing and seen status to the people you chat with.
  • To send message alerts you asked for.
  • To limit spam and abuse, keep the service secure and fix faults.
  • To show ads, as described above.

Who can see what

Everyone in a direct message or group can read its messages. A person added to a group can read the messages sent before they joined. People can find you only by typing your exact username, and there is no public list of users. Your display name and picture are visible to people you chat with.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your messages or account details. We share information only with:

  • Our hosting and database providers, who store and run the service for us.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google, and for advertising as described above.
  • Browser push services run by Google, Mozilla, Apple and Microsoft, if you turn on notifications.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and direct or group messages are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Disappearing messages are deleted after ten seconds.
  • Login sessions end after 14 days.
  • The public room is held in memory and is not saved.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing 2008madhesh@gmail.com from the contact address you use or with your username. We aim to reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 may give you further rights, including the right to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority.

In the app you can also log out, turn notifications off, turn on disappear mode, and choose or change your picture.

Children

Atti chat is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored as hashes, login cookies cannot be read by scripts, and the live site is served over HTTPS. No service is completely secure, and messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so do not share anything in a chat that you would not want stored on a server.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: 2008madhesh@gmail.com, or the contact page.
